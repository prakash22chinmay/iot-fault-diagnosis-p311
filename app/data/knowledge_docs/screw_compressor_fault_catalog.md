# Rotary Screw Air Compressor Fault Catalog & Diagnosis

## 1. Fault Code: E601_COMPRESSOR_AIREND_OVERTEMP
- **Primary Symptoms**:
  - Airend discharge temperature exceeds 100°C (typical shutdown limit 109°C / 228°F).
  - High oil carryover in compressed air distribution lines.
  - Thermal trip indicator activated.
- **Root Causes**:
  - Low synthetic compressor lubricant level or degraded lubricant viscosity.
  - Oil cooler matrix clogged with environmental dust, oil film, or pollen.
  - Thermal mixing valve (thermostatic bypass valve) stuck in bypass position, preventing oil flow to the radiator cooler.
  - Ambient room temperature exceeding 46°C with insufficient ventilation louvers.
- **Prescribed Remediation**:
  1. Check oil sight glass with machine shut down and depressurized; top off with OEM approved rotary lubricant (e.g. ISO VG 46/68 synthetic).
  2. Inspect oil cooler radiator fins; blow out dust from reverse direction with low-pressure compressed air (max 2 bar).
  3. Remove and test thermostatic valve element in heated oil bath; ensure full stroke at nominal actuation temperature (71°C).
  4. Replace air-oil separator element if differential pressure exceeds 0.8 bar (12 psi).

## 2. Fault Code: E605_ROTOR_RUB_AND_BEARING_PLAY
- **Primary Symptoms**:
  - High vibration RMS > 7.0 mm/s (Zone D), with sharp metallic scuffing acoustic frequency.
  - High motor current during loaded compression cycle.
- **Prescribed Remediation**:
  1. Immediately stop compressor to prevent catastrophic screw seizure.
  2. Perform axial and radial dial gauge deflection test on male and female rotor shafts.
  3. Send airend to certified rebuilder if rotor clearance is lost.
