# ISO 10816-3 Mechanical Vibration Standard Reference Guide

## 1. Overview and Scope
The ISO 10816-3 international standard establishes evaluation criteria for vibration severity of industrial machines with nominal power above 15 kW and operating speeds between 120 RPM and 15,000 RPM. Measurement is conducted as vibration velocity Root Mean Square (RMS) in mm/s over the 10 Hz to 1000 Hz frequency range.

## 2. Machine Classification Categories
- **Group 1**: Large machines (rated power > 300 kW; electrical machines with shaft height H >= 315 mm).
- **Group 2**: Medium-sized industrial machines (rated power 15 kW to 300 kW; electrical machines with shaft height 160 mm <= H < 315 mm).
- **Subclass Rigid Support**: Machine bolted to heavy reinforced concrete slab.
- **Subclass Flexible Support**: Machine mounted on springs, rubber elastomeric isolators, or light steel frame.

## 3. Vibration Velocity Severity Zones (RMS mm/s)
- **Zone A (Good / Excellent)**:
  - Vibration of newly commissioned or overhauled machines.
  - Limits: Group 1 Rigid: < 2.3 mm/s | Group 1 Flexible: < 3.5 mm/s | Group 2 Rigid: < 1.4 mm/s | Group 2 Flexible: < 2.3 mm/s.
- **Zone B (Acceptable / Unrestricted Long-Term Operation)**:
  - Machines with vibration in this zone are considered acceptable for unrestricted continuous operation without time limit.
  - Limits: Group 1 Rigid: 2.3 - 4.5 mm/s | Group 1 Flexible: 3.5 - 7.1 mm/s | Group 2 Rigid: 1.4 - 2.8 mm/s | Group 2 Flexible: 2.3 - 4.5 mm/s.
- **Zone C (Unsatisfactory / Action Required)**:
  - Vibration is considered unsatisfactory for continuous long-term operation. Machine may operate for a limited period until an opportunity arises for remedial maintenance.
  - Limits: Group 1 Rigid: 4.5 - 7.1 mm/s | Group 1 Flexible: 7.1 - 11.0 mm/s | Group 2 Rigid: 2.8 - 4.5 mm/s | Group 2 Flexible: 4.5 - 7.1 mm/s.
- **Zone D (Unacceptable / Critical Danger)**:
  - Vibration severity is high enough to cause severe structural failure, bearing destruction, or catastrophic rotor seizure.
  - Group 1: > 7.1 mm/s (Rigid) or > 11.0 mm/s (Flexible).
  - Group 2: > 4.5 mm/s (Rigid) or > 7.1 mm/s (Flexible).
  - Action: Immediate shutdown and root cause investigation.

## 4. Peak Acceleration Criteria for Rolling Element Bearings
- **Normal baseline**: Peak acceleration < 0.8 g.
- **Early sub-surface micro-spalling**: 0.8 g to 1.8 g (ultrasonic stress waves detectable).
- **Advanced bearing defect (flaking/raceway pit)**: 1.8 g to 3.5 g.
- **Imminent bearing seizure**: > 3.5 g with bearing casing temperature > 80°C.
