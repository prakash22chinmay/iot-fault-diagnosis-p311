# Industrial FMEA Troubleshooting & Plant Safety Protocols

## 1. Golden Rules of Safety for Machine Maintenance
1. **Lockout / Tagout (LOTO - OSHA 29 CFR 1910.147)**:
   - Always de-energize and lock the primary circuit breaker using an individual padlock and danger tag before removing any guards or opening terminal enclosures.
   - Verify zero energy state: test with a calibrated multimeter on incoming phases before touching conductors.
2. **Pressurized Fluid Systems**:
   - Never crack a flange or disconnect hoses on pressurized pump or compressor lines.
   - Always verify pressure gauges read 0.0 Bar and open manual bleed/drain valves prior to servicing.
3. **Rotating Machinery Hazard**:
   - Ensure shafts and couplings have come to a complete mechanical standstill before removing safety guards.
   - Wear snug safety glasses, steel-toed boots, and cut-resistant gloves. Avoid loose jewelry, ties, or dangling hair.

## 2. Standard Diagnostic Decision Matrix
- **Condition: High Vibration (> 7.1 mm/s) + High Temperature (> 85°C)**:
  - Diagnosis: Catastrophic bearing degradation or loss of lubrication.
  - Urgency: IMMEDIATE SHUTDOWN (< 1 hour).
- **Condition: High Vibration (> 5.0 mm/s) + Normal Temperature (< 65°C)**:
  - Diagnosis: Mechanical unbalance, structural looseness, or angular misalignment.
  - Urgency: Schedule dynamic balancing or laser alignment on next planned outage.
- **Condition: Erratic Pressure Fluctuations + Crackling Sound + Normal Current**:
  - Diagnosis: Hydraulic Cavitation or suction line obstruction.
  - Urgency: Clear suction line or throttle discharge immediately.
- **Condition: High Temperature (> 90°C) + High Current (> 115% FLA) + Normal Vibration**:
  - Diagnosis: Stator overload, restricted cooling ventilation, or phase voltage imbalance.
  - Urgency: Clean cooling shroud and verify 3-phase supply balance within 2 hours.
